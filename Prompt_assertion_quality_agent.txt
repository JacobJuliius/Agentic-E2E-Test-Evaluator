1. Scan for Verifications: Identify all assertion statements in the provided executable_test_step_code.
2. Cross-reference with Requirements: Read the provided fine_grained_reqs. What is the actual business outcome expected by the user?

   - Missing Assertions: Actions were performed (like submitting a form or adding to cart), but there is NO corresponding assertion to verify the result of that action.

- fine_grained_reqs: The business requirements context.
- excutable_test_step_code: The generated Playwright test script.

  "missing_assertions_rationale": "<If actions exist without verification, describe exactly what should have been asserted>"
